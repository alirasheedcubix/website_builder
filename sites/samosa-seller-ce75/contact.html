Enter from the Hawa Mahal side. Pass the cloth shops. After the blue door, we are the next arch. The lane is for walking.

A note for the house

Tell us before noon.

For a dozen, a table larger than six, or a question the board does not answer. Your words stay on this device.

Before you come

Is the keema kept separate?

Yes. It has a kettle of its own. The other folds, including mawa, are vegetarian.

How hot is it?

The samosa itself is gentle. The red chilli oil is the heat, and it stays on the side unless you ask.

Can you make a dozen?

Yes. Leave a note or call before noon. We deliver on foot inside the old city, and we stop before dusk.

Do you take a card?

Cash and UPI at the arch. The note form does not take payment.
