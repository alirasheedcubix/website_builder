01 — The folds

Five folds, two kettles.

Keema fries on its own. The other four, and the sweet, stay in the vegetarian kettle.

Three chutneys, ground this morning.

They arrive with every plate. The red chilli oil can stay in the kitchen.

  • 01

    Hari

    Mint, coriander, and green chilli. Cool, and a little sharp.

  • 02

    Imli

    Tamarind, jaggery, and roasted cumin. The sweet-sour one.

  • 03

    Lal tel

    Dried red chilli in mustard oil. Say if you would rather not.

02 — How a batch happens

From dough to paper cone.

  1. 01

    Rest

    Flour, a little ajwain, and water. The dough sits. We do not rush it.

  2. 02

    Fill

    The potato is dry enough to hold a shape. The keema has already been simmered with clove.

  3. 03

    Fold

    A triangle, sealed with water and the side of a thumb. The edge is the whole craft.

  4. 04

    Fry

    Groundnut oil, a medium flame, turned once. Lifted when the crust will shatter.
